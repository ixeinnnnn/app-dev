## Overview 
Ready Player One is a science fiction adventure film directed by Steven Spielberg, released in 2018.
It is based on the novel by Ernest Cline and set in a dystopian future where people escape reality through a virtual world called the OASIS.

## Plot twist 
*"Reality is the only thing that's real"* 

In the year 2045, much of humanity spends its time in the OASIS, a massive virtual reality universe.
The story follows Wade Watts, a teenager who embarks on a quest to find an Easter egg left by the OASIS creator, which grants full control of the virtual world.

## Main Characters

1. First item Wade Watts / Parzival – The main protagonist and egg hunter (played by Tye Sheridan).
2. Second item Art3mis / Samantha Cook – Wade’s friend and love interest (played by Olivia Cooke).
3. Third item Aech, Shoto, and Daito – Wade’s allies in the quest.
4. fourth item Nolan Sorrento – The main antagonist from the IOI corporation.

### Favorite Things About the Movie

- First item he visual effects are stunning, blending real-world and VR scenes seamlessly.
- Second item The film is packed with pop culture references from the ’80s and ’90s.
- Third item The soundtrack gives a nostalgic vibe that perfectly fits the theme.
- Fourth item The story celebrates friendship, teamwork, and the importance of reality.
